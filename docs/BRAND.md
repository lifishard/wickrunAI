# wickrunAI identity

The amber w is the wick: it stays upright while the surrounding open orbit turns. Use the flat mark at small sizes; keep clear space around it.

- Light surfaces: neutral ring and `#B8751A` wick.
- Dark surfaces: off-white ring and `#E8B45B` wick.
- Loading: 5.6-second orbit, 2.4-second gentle wick breathing. Reduced motion removes rotation.
- Startup: real data loading first, then three decreasing hops and a localized welcome. Escape or Enter app skips the welcome. Reduced motion uses a short fade.
